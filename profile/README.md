## Welcome to Rinble 👋

# Privacy Policy for Talk SIP Application

**Effective Date:** November 22, 2025

## Overview

Talk is a SIP (Session Initiation Protocol) communication application that enables voice calls over the internet. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our application.

## Information We Collect

### 1. Audio Data
- **What we collect:** Voice recordings during calls
- **How we collect:** Through your device's microphone with your explicit consent
- **Purpose:** To transmit your voice during SIP calls
- **Storage:** Audio data is transmitted in real-time and not stored permanently on our servers

### 2. Device Information
- **What we collect:** Device identifier, operating system version
- **Purpose:** To ensure compatibility and troubleshoot technical issues
- **Storage:** Stored locally on your device only

### 3. Call Metadata
- **What we collect:** Call timestamps, duration, phone numbers dialed
- **Purpose:** To establish and maintain SIP connections
- **Storage:** Minimal data stored locally for call history functionality

### 4. Authentication Information
- **What we collect:** Login credentials, SIP account details
- **Purpose:** To authenticate you with SIP services
- **Storage:** Encrypted storage on your device

## How We Use Your Information

- **Audio Recording:** Exclusively for real-time voice transmission during calls
- **Call Functionality:** To establish, maintain, and terminate SIP connections
- **Authentication:** To verify your identity with SIP services
- **Technical Support:** To diagnose and resolve technical issues

## Data Storage and Security

- Audio recordings are **not stored permanently** - they are transmitted in real-time during calls
- All sensitive data is encrypted using industry-standard encryption
- We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information
- No data is sold to third parties
- No data is used for advertising purposes

## Data Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties except as necessary:
- **SIP Service Providers:** To transmit voice calls over SIP networks
- **Service Providers:** Only essential technical partners required for app functionality

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access the information we collect about you
- Delete your account and associated data
- Request modification of your personal information
- Opt-out of data collection where technically feasible

## Children's Privacy

Our application is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:
- Updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy
- Providing notice within the application

## Contact Information

For questions about this privacy policy or to exercise your rights, please contact us:

**Email:** privacy@rinble.com

## Technical Implementation Details

### Audio Recording Process
- Audio is captured using Android's `RECORD_AUDIO` permission
- Audio is processed in real-time for SIP transmission
- No audio files are saved to device storage
- Audio transmission uses secure SIP protocols

### Data Minimization
- We collect only the minimum data necessary for call functionality
- No unnecessary data collection or storage
- Regular data cleanup and retention policies

### Compliance
- This policy complies with:
  - Google Play Developer Policies
  - Android permissions requirements
  - Basic privacy principles
